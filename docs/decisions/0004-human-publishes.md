# 0004. A human publishes

Status: accepted · 2026-11-05 · amended by 0007 when it is accepted

## Context

Nobody in the playbooks got paid for an unreviewed dump, and a funded post on the wrong identity stains it.

## Decision

Agents draft, call the rented tool, write job files, and log the measure. A human picks the strategy, vetoes the hook, uploads, and submits.

## Consequences

Publishing and marketplace logins happen on the operator's machine. A cloud agent cannot complete the day-14 proof and must not fake a URL.
