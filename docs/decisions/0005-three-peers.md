# 0005. Three peers; extensions are not the first proof

Status: accepted · 2026-11-05

## Context

Seven strategies written as peers produced a menu and no unit.

## Decision

Peers are `funded-clip`, `owned-derivative`, and `generated`. A job is exactly one, on its own identity. `text-pack`, `audio-native`, `procedural`, and `downstream-sku` are extensions; procedural and SKU wait for a proof.

## Consequences

`check.py` enforces one identity per strategy and the extension gate.
