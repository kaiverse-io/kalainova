The bar had a floor on how far music sits under a voice, so a score 43 dB under the words, which no viewer can hear, passed. The reading was right there and read as a success.

**Why:** a pass hides how far past the limit a value went. The film would have gone to review with no audible music.

**How to apply:** Give a reading both sides when both directions are faults. When reviewing a piece, read the numbers and look and listen too. In one pass, reading the pages found a balloon tail across a face, stranded lines, and stray words, none of which any check saw. When something is levelled, level it by the same number the bar reads.
