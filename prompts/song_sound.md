- "style" is a short production brief, not a list of keywords. Write it in this order: genre; emotion; rhythmic feel; the voice (its timbre, how close it sits, clear diction, the phrase it leans on); the core instruments, each with its role ("rolling sub bass carrying the groove"); production texture. Keep the three to five traits that matter most clear, and stay under 450 characters: a model follows a short brief better than a long one, and a vague result is fixed by cutting words, not adding them. Six core instruments at most. Never "in the style of" anyone.
- The tempo and key are numbers in "bpm" and "key"; the style never names them.
- Use only words for what you want, in "style" and in every section's "style": a model hears every sound named there, so "no autotune" asks for autotune. What to keep out goes in "avoid", in a few words. A section without a voice has no "lines", and its style says "instrumental".
- How the track moves goes in each section's "style": a few words, under 80 characters, for what changes there ("restrained and intimate", "drums drop out, pads and voice alone", "full groove returns, harmonies wide"). Never a bar number or a count: the model is sent no bars, only the words, the style and the length. A section that must leave something out, such as the drums of a soft intro or a breakdown, names it in that section's "avoid"; a model that takes it per section gets it there. A model that reads notes on lyric tags gets each one on its section's tag; any other model gets them after the style. Two takes of one style come back with the same beat when nothing says how the sections differ.
- "pronounce": every word or name the lines sing that is not of the song's "language", or is uncommon, with how it is said, spelled so a reader of the song's language says it right ({"word as written": "how it sounds"}). Leave it out when there are none.
- "energy" on every section, 1 to 5: how much it carries, from 1 (nearly nothing: a filtered pad, a lone motif) to 5 (the song's fullest moment). It is your plan of the song's arc, and the take is checked against it section by section, so choose it for this song, not by habit.
